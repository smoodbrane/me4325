# me4325

## About
I am an engineering student at the University of Limerick, undertaking a bachelor's degree in Digital Mechatronic Engineering.

My academic focus reflects the interdisciplinary nature of Digital Mechatronic Engineering, spanning embedded systems, control theory, and mechanical design. I am particularly interested in the integration of digital electronics, software, and physical systems, from initial design through to a functioning mechatronic implementation. In addition to my coursework, I undertake independent projects to further develop these skills.

I am currently working as an engineering intern at Aerogen, a med-tech company in a process development role.

## Portfolio

- **Automated Sorting Conveyor:** Designed and built, as part of a team, an automated sorting conveyor capable of detecting part heights and rejecting out of spec parts into a dedicated reject bin. The project incorporated pneumatics and PLC programming. My primary role was design, using CAD to develop 3D-printed components. Our design won a competition judged by DesignPro Automation, a med-tech automation company.
- **Model Bike Shelter:** Designed and built a functional electromechanical model of an underground bike storage facility, incorporating mechanisms for lifting and lowering the platform, and for moving the bike onto and off of a storage rack.
- **ESP32 RC Car:** A remote-controlled car built from the ground up, with all control logic, wireless communication and motor control programmed on an ESP32 microcontroller.
