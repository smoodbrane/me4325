# Recommendation Report Outline

## 1. Background
Explains what aperture plates are, why batches must be kept long term, and why current pallet storage will not keep up with the new machine's output.

## 2. Recommendation: Store Aperture Plates in Labelled Vials
States the recommendation up front: adopt labelled vials, held in racks, as the long term stage of a three stage storage lifecycle.

## 3. Limitations of Current Pallet Storage
Sets out the problems with pallets as a long term store: capacity, storage density and retrieval effort.

## 4. Criteria for Selecting a Storage Method
Defines the four evaluation criteria (storage density, protection, biocompatibility and traceability) and how they are weighted.

## 5. How Vials Meet Each Criterion
Presents the evidence for vials, with one subsection per criterion and a final subsection on the storage lifecycle.

## 6. Alternatives Considered and Why Vials Are Preferred
Compares pallets, cut tubing and vials in a table, and explains why each alternative was not 
chosen.

## 7. Managing the Transfer to Vials
Identifies the risks of moving batches into vials and how they will be reduced.

## 8. Next Steps to Introduce Vial Storage
Lists the actions needed to approve, trial and introduce vial storage.

## 9. Benefits of Adopting Vial Storage
Summarises the benefits and closes with a call to approve the order.
