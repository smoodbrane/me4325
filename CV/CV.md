## Projects and Volunteering

### Underground Bike Storage Model, Leaving Certificate Technology Project

The brief was to build a functional electromechanical model of something that helps people in a public space. After researching options, I used CAD to design an underground bike storage system. The model used a linkage system to raise and lower the main platform and a servo motor to move bikes on and off the racks, all controlled by a button-operated microcontroller.

I volunteered to help turn a derelict garden at my secondary school into a sensory garden. I was involved in the initial planning, as well as clearing, weeding and planting new flowers.
